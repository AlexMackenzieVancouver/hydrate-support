Common Solutions

  • Restore purchases: Open Hydrate Settings, then tap Restore Purchases.
  • Apple Health: Check the Health app under Data Access & Devices to confirm Hydrate has permission.
  • Apple Watch: Open the Watch app on your iPhone, then install Hydrate from My Watch.
