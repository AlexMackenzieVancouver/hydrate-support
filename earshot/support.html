Common questions

  • Notes came out as plain extraction instead of a proper summary. The on-device AI model needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer and Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other iPhones, Earshot uses plain extraction instead — accurate, just less fluent. It tells you when that happens.
  • Speakers aren't named. Tap any speaker label in the transcript and give it a name. Earshot remembers that voice and names it automatically in future conversations.
  • A speaker was named wrong. Tap the label and correct it. The fix applies to the whole conversation and to every future one.
  • Action items didn't reach Reminders. Accept the items first, then tap Send to Reminders. Check that Earshot has Reminders access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Reminders.
  • Apple Watch capture hasn't appeared on my iPhone. Open Earshot on your iPhone with the Watch nearby. Transfers resume automatically after being out of range; the Watch shows how many captures are still waiting and does not delete audio until the iPhone confirms it arrived.
  • The app stopped mid-recording. Reopen Earshot — the audio is written to disk continuously, so an unfinished recording is offered back to you at the top of the list.
  • Restore purchases. Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Getting my notes out. Export to Markdown from any note, or export everything from Settings. Export is never restricted by plan.

What's free
