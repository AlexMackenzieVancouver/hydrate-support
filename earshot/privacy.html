Earshot — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Earshot does not collect your data. Not in an anonymized way, not in an aggregated way, not to improve our models. There is no server to collect it to.

What stays on your iPhone

All of it. Recordings, transcripts, your typed notes, AI-generated summaries, action items, and speaker voice prints are stored on your device and processed there.

  • Transcription runs on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not sent to Apple or to us.
  • Summaries and action items are generated by Apple's on-device model. Your transcript is not sent anywhere to produce them.
  • Voice prints used to recognise a speaker across meetings never leave your device, are never included in exports, and are deleted when you delete that speaker.

You can verify this: turn on Airplane Mode and use the whole app.

What can leave, and only when you say so

Earshot never sends anything anywhere on its own. Content leaves your device only when you choose a destination and confirm exactly what is being sent:

  • Reminders, Calendar, Files, Mail drafts — these stay on your device and sync only through your own iCloud account, under Apple's terms, not ours.
  • Webhooks or your own cloud AI key — optional, off by default, and every use shows you the payload and destination first.

Every one of these writes an entry to the Privacy Ledger inside the app, so you can always see what left, when, and where it went. For most people that screen stays empty.

Training

We have never trained a model on anything you said, wrote, or recorded, and there is no setting to change that. There is no setting because there is nothing to turn off.

Analytics and tracking

None. Earshot contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK beyond Apple's own MetricKit, no advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many meetings you record, how long they are, or whether you opened the app today.

Accounts and purchases

Earshot does not require an account. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your name, email, or payment details.

Recording others

Recording laws vary depending on where you and the people you are talking to are. Earshot flags places where everyone usually has to agree, and gives you a sentence to say out loud, but this is not legal advice and the responsibility for recording lawfully is yours.

Deleting your data

Delete a note in the app and its audio, transcript, and notes are removed from your device. Delete the app and everything goes with it. There is no copy anywhere else for us to delete on your behalf.
