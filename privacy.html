Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Hydrate is built privacy-first. We designed it so that your hydration data stays with you. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

No account, no sign-in

Hydrate does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or any login. We do not have a server that stores your hydration history.

Where your data lives

  • Your logs, goals, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • If you enable iCloud, your data syncs privately through your own Apple iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). We never see it.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Apple Health (optional)

If you grant permission, Hydrate can:

  • Read step count and active energy, used only to raise your daily goal on more active days.
  • Write the water you log to Apple Health, so it stays in sync with the rest of your health data.

This data flows only between Hydrate and Apple Health on your device. It is never sent to us. You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Health app.

Location (optional)

If you enable weather-based goals, Hydrate requests your approximate location solely to ask Apple Weather for the local temperature, so it can adjust your goal on hot days. Your location is used in the moment and is not stored and never leaves your device. You can decline or revoke this at any time.

Diagnostics (off by default)

Hydrate does not include advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. Optional, anonymous diagnostic events (to help us fix crashes and improve reliability) are turned off by default. They are only sent if you explicitly opt in under Settings → Privacy, contain no personal identifiers, and can be turned off again at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Hydrate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll revise the date above and post the new version here.
