Common questions

Why doesn't coffee count fully? It does count — just weighted by the Beverage Hydration Index (Maughan et al., 2016). Coffee credits ~92% of its volume toward your goal.

Why did my goal change? Hydrate adapts your daily goal to hot weather and active days. You can see the reason on the Today screen.

The Apple Watch app isn't installed. Open the Watch app on your iPhone → My Watch → scroll to Hydrate → Install.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings → Hydrate Pro → Restore Purchases.
